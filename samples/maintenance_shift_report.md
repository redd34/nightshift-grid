# Plant 3 Night Shift Maintenance Report

## Compressor line

During the 02:00 shift handover, the vibration monitor on Compressor Turbine CT-7 reported 4.6 mm/s on PLC-Node-3, above the 4.2 mm/s alarm threshold. The duty technician, R. Okafor, isolated the turbine and replaced bearing assembly B-131. Modbus register 40012 was recalibrated after the swap and vibration settled at 1.9 mm/s.

Lubrication pump LP-2 feeding CT-7 showed a pressure drop to 2.1 bar. The pump strainer was cleaned and pressure recovered to 3.4 bar.

## Packaging line

Conveyor C-12 tripped twice on overcurrent. Motor M-12 was inspected by technician L. Varga, who found a worn drive belt and replaced it. PLC-Node-1 logged both trips with fault code F-221.

## Follow-ups

- Order spare bearing assembly B-131 for the CT-7 critical spares shelf.
- Schedule thermography on Motor M-12 next week.
